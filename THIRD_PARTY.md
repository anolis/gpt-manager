# Third-party components

The optional cloud connector is [rclone](https://rclone.org/), developed by Nick Craig-Wood and contributors and distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/rclone/rclone/blob/master/COPYING). GPT Manager downloads its official pinned release on first account connection and retains its bundled README alongside the executable. Binaries are not committed to this repository.

Electron, xterm.js, and the fit addon retain their licenses in their installed npm packages. The project’s `package-lock.json` records their versions and integrity hashes.
